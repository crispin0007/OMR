## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `OMR-Setup-<version>.exe` (installer) or `OMR-Portable-<version>.zip` (no installing) |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) | `OMR-Checker-<version>-macos-apple-silicon.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `OMR-Checker-<version>-macos-intel.dmg` |
| Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint | `omr-checker_<version>_amd64.deb` |
| Any other Linux | `OMR-Checker-<version>-x86_64.AppImage`, or the `.tar.gz` |

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

**The first time you open it:** these downloads are not signed with a paid certificate.
- **Windows:** if "Windows protected your PC" appears, press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** right-click the app in Applications and choose **Open**. On macOS 15 or newer, if it is
  blocked, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and press **Open Anyway**.
- **AppImage:** make it runnable first (right-click > Properties > Allow executing, or
  `chmod +x OMR-Checker-*.AppImage`).

Scanning straight from a scanner's feeder works on Windows. On Mac and Linux, scan into a folder
with the scanner's own software and let OMR Checker watch it, or import the scans.
