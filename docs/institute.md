# Your institute: name, logo and letterhead

[Guide home](index.md) · Previous: [Send results by email or SMS](sending.md) · Next: [Backup, restore and settings](backup-and-settings.md)

Tell the app your institute's name, logo and details once. They are then printed as a letterhead
on every scorecard and student report, put at the top of the Excel merit list, shown on the exam's
page, and filled in for you on new answer sheets.

![The Institutes window with a live scorecard preview](images/institutes.png)

## Add your institute

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar and press **Institutes…** on the *Institute* row.
2. Type the **Name**. Everything else is optional: address, phone, email, website, motto, who signs
   the scorecards and their title, and a footer line (for example "Results are provisional").
3. Press **Choose logo…** and pick a PNG or JPEG. The app trims empty margins and makes it the right
   size. A logo with a transparent background looks best. It refuses a picture that is empty, very
   small, or very long and thin, and tells you why.
4. Press the colour button to pick the institute's colour. It is used for the name and the line
   under the letterhead.
5. Press **Save**.

The preview on the right is a real scorecard with made-up marks. It changes as you type, so you can
see how a long name or a dark colour looks before printing anything.

## More than one institute

If you run more than one branch or school, press **New** and add each one. The first is the
**default**: every exam uses it unless you pick another. **Make default** changes which one that is.
**Delete…** removes an institute; its exams use the default one instead. Exams and results are never
touched.

To use a different institute for one exam, open the exam's **Exam settings** tab and pick it under
**Institute**. The exam's page shows that institute's logo and name at the top.

## Where it appears

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| Scorecards (PDF) | Letterhead with logo, name, address and contact; signature line and footer |
| Student reports (HTML and PDF) | The same letterhead, in the institute's colour |
| Merit list (Excel) | "Institute: Exam name" at the top, in the institute's colour |
| New exam | Heading, colour and logo filled in from the default institute |
| The exam page | Logo and name at the top |

A **CSV** file has no letterhead: it is plain columns for other programs, and an extra line at the
top would break them.

## Backups

The institutes are saved in backups. Restoring a backup on another PC brings them along, without
making a second copy of one that is already there.
