# FAQ

[Guide home](index.md) · Previous: [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) · Next: [Cheat sheet](cheat-sheet.md)

**Is OMR Checker free?**
Yes, completely. It is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with no per-sheet or yearly fee.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. It works fully offline and never uploads sheets or results.

**Which scanners work?**
Any scanner Windows can scan with (through WIA). One with a feeder (ADF) is fastest. Others work
through a folder; see [Scan the filled sheets](scanning.md#2-from-a-folder).

**Can I use a phone instead of a scanner?**
For a few sheets, yes: take straight photos and use **Import scans…**. For a whole class a scanner
is faster and more reliable.

**How fast is it?**
It reads about 250 sheets a minute, which is usually faster than the scanner feeds them.

**Do I have to enter the answer key before scanning?**
No. Scan whenever you like; scores appear as soon as the key is in.

**Can I change the marks after scanning?**
Yes. Change them on **Marks and sections** and every score is worked out again.

**What if a student fills two bubbles?**
It goes to [Review](review.md), where you decide; press **X** to mark it invalid. The app never
guesses. Whether an invalid answer scores nothing or the wrong marks is set on
[Marks](marks-and-sections.md#two-marks-on-one-question).

**Can students have different question paper sets?**
Yes, up to 8 sets, with an answer key each. The student fills their set on the sheet.

**Can I use the sheets my school already prints?**
Yes; map them once with [Map printed sheet](map-printed-sheet.md).

**Can I print on A4 and Letter?**
Yes, and also Legal, A3 and A5. The sheet reads the same on all of them.

**What are sheet numbers for?**
To find a missing or repeated sheet after scanning. They are optional; see
[Sheet numbers](print-sheets.md#sheet-numbers).

**Are the NEET, JEE, MECEE and other templates the official sheets?**
No. They follow those exams' question pattern and marking, on OMR Checker's own sheet design.

**Where is my data, and how do I move it to a new PC?**
See [Backup, restore and settings](backup-and-settings.md).

**I found a problem. Where do I report it?**
Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Please don't attach scans of real students' sheets.
