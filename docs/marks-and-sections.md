# Marks, sections, topics and written marks

[Guide home](index.md) · Previous: [The answer key](answer-key.md) · Next: [Review](review.md)

## Marks

Open the exam's **Marks and sections** tab.

![The Marks and sections tab](images/marks-and-sections.png)

- **Correct**: marks for a right answer, for example 4.
- **Wrong**: marks taken off for a wrong answer (**negative marking**). Type the number taken off,
  for example 0.25 or 1. Type 0 for no negative marking.
- **Blank**: marks for a question left empty, usually 0.

You can change the marks at any time, even after scanning. Every score is worked out again at once.

## Two marks on one question

Your exam rules already say what two marks on a question that takes one answer mean, so the app
can follow them instead of asking you on every sheet. Under **Two marks where one answer is asked
for**:

- **Two filled bubbles on one question are**
  - **invalid, without Review** (the default for new exams): no one checks them;
  - **sent to Review**: you decide each one.
- **A filled bubble with a faint second mark is** (a light scribble, or a mark not quite rubbed out)
  - **sent to Review** (the default): you decide, and press **X** for Invalid;
  - **invalid, without Review**;
  - **left out: the filled bubble is the answer**.
- **An invalid answer scores**
  - **no marks** (the default): no marks, and no negative marks either;
  - **the wrong marks**: the same as a wrong answer, negative marking included.

A rule covers every sheet already scanned as well. Change it back to **sent to Review** and those
questions go back to Review as they were read.

What a rule settled is listed under the Review queue, not in it, so you can glance down the list,
click one to see that part of the sheet, and change it if you need to.

Before choosing **invalid** or **left out** for faint marks, think about erasing. If students rub
out an answer to change it and the old bubble still shows, *invalid* voids their answer and *left
out* takes whichever bubble the reader counted as filled. A faint mark on its own, with no filled bubble, always goes
to Review.

Exams made before this rule keep sending both kinds to Review until you change them here.

## Sections

A **section** is a group of questions with its own name and marks, for example *Physics, questions
1 to 30, 4 right, −1 wrong*. Results then show a score per section.

Press **Sections…**, then **Add section** for each one and fill in its name, first and last
question and marks. **Remove section** deletes one. Questions in no section use the exam's marks.

![The Sections window](images/sections-dialog.png)

## Topics

A **topic** is a label for some questions, such as *Kinematics* or *Algebra*. It helps a student
see what to study.

Press **Topics…** and type one line per topic: `1-10: Kinematics`. Then:

- every scorecard shows the share of each topic the student got right;
- the Excel merit list gets a **Topics** sheet, with a row per student and one for everyone.

## Written marks

Some exams have a written (subjective) part marked by a teacher. Open **Written marks**:

1. Set **Written part out of**, for example 20.
2. Type each student's marks.

They are added to the OMR score for the rank. Results and scorecards show both parts.
