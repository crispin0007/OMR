# Quick start: your first exam in 5 steps

[Guide home](index.md) · Next: [Create an exam](create-an-exam.md)

Try this first with five practice sheets that you fill in yourself. Then you'll know the whole job
before a real exam.

## 1. Create the exam

On **Exams**, press **New exam…** (or **Ctrl+N**).

- Under **Start from**, pick a pattern close to your exam (for example *50-question class test*),
  or leave it blank.
- Press **Next** through the steps and change anything you like: the name, the number of questions,
  the marks.
- On the last step, press **Create exam**.

![New exam, first step, starting from a ready-made pattern](images/new-exam-start.png)

More: [Create an exam](create-an-exam.md).

## 2. Print the sheets

Open the exam's **Print sheet** tab. Press **Print…** to print, or **Save as PDF…** to print later
or at a print shop.

Before printing a hundred copies, print one, fill a few bubbles, scan or photograph it, and press
**Check a printed copy…**. It tells you whether that sheet reads.

![The Print sheet tab](images/print-sheet.png)

More: [Print the answer sheets](print-sheets.md).

## 3. Enter the answer key

Open **Answer key** and click the right option for each question, or just type the letters
(A, B, C…). The next question is picked for you after each one.

More: [The answer key](answer-key.md).

## 4. Scan the filled sheets

Put the filled sheets face down in the scanner's feeder and press **▶ Start scanning**. You'll
find this button in three places: first on the **Scan** tab, at the top right of every exam page,
and on the **Dashboard**.

Each sheet appears on screen as it is read, with right answers ringed in green and wrong ones in red.

No scanner? Press **Import scans…** and pick PDFs or photos of the sheets instead.

![Sheets being read live from the scanner](images/live-scanning.png)

More: [Scan the filled sheets](scanning.md).

## 5. Check and share the results

- Open **Review**. It lists only what needs a person: unclear marks and pages that couldn't be read.
  Press the right letter for each one.
- Open **Results**. Press **Merit list (Excel)…** for the ranked list, or **Scorecards (PDF)…**
  for one page per student.

![Results ranked by score](images/results.png)

More: [Review](review.md) and [Results](results.md).

That's it. Everything is saved as you go, so you can close the app at any time and carry on later.
