# Review: checking unclear marks

[Guide home](index.md) · Previous: [Marks, sections, topics and written marks](marks-and-sections.md) · Next: [Students](students.md)

OMR Checker never guesses a mark. When it isn't sure, it puts the item on the **Review** tab for a
person to decide. Everything else is already done.

![The Review tab showing part of a sheet with an unclear bubble boxed](images/review.png)

## What lands in Review

- A bubble **half filled**, **faint**, or **partly erased**.
- **Two bubbles** filled where only one is allowed.
- A **roll number** or **set** that can't be read clearly.
- The **same roll number** on more than one sheet.
- A roll number that isn't on your [student list](students.md).
- A **page that couldn't be read** at all (for example a corner circle cut off).

## How to fix an item

The list on the left shows every item. The right side shows **that part of the sheet**, with the
question boxed, and what it was **Read as**. The part of the sheet shown is marked in the same
colours as [Scanned sheets](scanning.md#after-scanning-scanned-sheets), with a thin circle where each bubble was
read, so you can see at once whether the mark sits in its bubble.

- Press the **letter** of the right answer (or click its button).
- Press **Delete** for **Blank**.
- Press **X** for **Invalid**: two or more marks on a question that takes one answer, even when the
  second is only a light scribble. It scores what the exam's [Marks](marks-and-sections.md#two-marks-on-one-question)
  tab says for two marks, and shows as *invalid* on the scorecard, in Excel, and as a red box on the
  marked sheet.
- Press **Space** for **Keep as read**.
- A numeric answer: type the number and press **Save number**.
- A roll number: type it and press **Save roll number**.

The next item opens by itself, so you can work through the list with the keyboard. Scores update
at once.

When the list is empty you'll see **Nothing to check. Results are ready.**

## Every change is kept

The **Changes made** box lists each correction: when, who, which sheet, which question, and the old
and new answer. Nothing is changed silently.

## A page that couldn't be read

Scan it again (straight, with all four corner circles on the page), or import the scan again. If
students filled a different sheet from the exam's, see
[Use a sheet you already print](map-printed-sheet.md).
